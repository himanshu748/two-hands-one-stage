2 · Try the scene

Read first. Then try it with the timer.

Say the large text aloud. Follow the numbered actions in order. There is no recorded voice or sound.

Ready when you are · five-second countdown

Need to stop? Set your objects down safely, then press Pause or Escape. Switching away also pauses. Put everything back before starting over. The timer is a guide, not a test.
